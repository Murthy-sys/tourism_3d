Nature surfaces and HDR daylight
================================
The following source assets are CC0 1.0 Universal, by Poly Haven:
https://polyhaven.com/license

rock: Rock Face 03 https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03
grass: Aerial Grass Rock https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
soil: Forest Ground 04 https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04
bark: Bark Willow https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow
wood: Wooden Planks https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_planks
sky: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Pure Sky
  https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
environment: Forest Slope https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_slope

The leaf color, opacity and normal are Leaf001 by ambientCG, CC0:
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leaf001
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Color, OpenGL normal, and combined AO/roughness/metalness maps were
resized/recompressed from the providers' 1K originals for web delivery.
Sky is the unmodified 1K source; the reflection HDR is averaged in linear
radiance to 512 px. Leaf UVs select one side of the atlas.
No provider preview images are redistributed.
Reproduction: scripts/prepare-nature-assets.mjs.
